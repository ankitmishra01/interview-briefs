# FitCheck Coach Roadmap

## Current

- Practise generic role-specific interview questions.
- Hear questions and feedback through browser text-to-speech.
- Dictate answers where browser speech recognition is supported.
- Score responses for JD fit, proof, structure, concision, and French delivery.
- Track local progress by role, language, and question.

## Next

- Add adaptive follow-up questions when answers are vague, missing proof, or too long.
- Add session summaries that identify the strongest answer, weakest answer, and next drill.
- Add a job-description importer that turns pasted JDs into practice questions and scoring terms.
- Improve French coaching with rewritten answer scripts at B1/B2/C1 levels.

## Later

- Add optional AI Gateway-powered interviewer turns when account credentials are configured.
- Add exportable practice reports.
- Add shareable anonymized demo sessions.
- Add custom role packs for sales, product, delivery, founder, and policy interviews.

## Product Principles

- Local-first by default.
- No private interview data in the public demo.
- Useful without paid AI services.
- French feedback should be practical, spoken, and interview-safe rather than academic.
